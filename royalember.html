The Discipline of Fire.

We don't cook. We control decomposition through heat. At Ember, we rely exclusively on open wood fires. No gas. No electric ovens. We source seasoned hickory, white oak, and cherry wood, reducing them to glowing coals before they ever touch our ingredients. The ash is our seasoning; the smoke is our sauce. Welcome to the hearth.

Why We Burn Hickory

Not all smoke is created equal. The resin content of a tree dictates the bitterness of the exhaust. We traveled through three states to find the specific strain of hickory that provides a sweet, slow-burning ember without leaving an acrid film on the palate.
